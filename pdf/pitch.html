How it works · the smart damper (concept sketch)

A magnet moving through a coil: power and ride control in one part.

1 · A bump moves it
Track bump → coach moves → magnet slides through the coil
2 · It makes power
e = Ke · v (Faraday's law) → charges the bogie power bank
3 · It sets the ride
c = Ke² ÷ (R coil + R load) → firmness set in milliseconds
4 · It fails safe
Power lost → relay → resistor → works like a normal damper
Fits existing damper mounts · no oil · no gears
